Contributor links

Let the band do the typing.

Give the headliner’s tour manager, the support act and the promoter each a private link. They add their own guests, see exactly how many spots are left, and it locks itself at your cutoff.

  • Allocations counted in heads — “Jane +2” uses 3
  • No login or app for contributors — just the link
  • They only ever see their own list
  • Switch a link off, or replace it, in one tap

Door mode

A door that keeps up with the queue.

Big search, big buttons. Type two letters, tap IN. Parties arrive in bits, people step out and come back, and every phone on the door updates the moment anyone taps.

  • Partial plus-ones — “2 of 3 in”
  • Check out and back in, without double counting
  • Walk-ups added straight from the search
  • Door notes pinned for the whole shift
  • Gold highlight and an alert on every door for VIPs

Accountability built in

Every tap has a name on it.

There are no staff accounts to manage. Each phone asks for a name once — so the activity log shows exactly who added whom, who let them in, and when. Export the whole night to CSV for settlement.

  • One venue login, shared by staff
  • Full history of adds, edits, check-ins and check-outs
  • Paste lists straight from Excel, Sheets or an email

Questions

Do our staff need accounts?

No. Your venue has one username and password. Staff open your venue’s link, type the password, and put their name on the phone once.

Do artists and promoters need to sign up?

No. They get a private link for that show. They can add, edit and remove their own guests until your cutoff — and they only ever see their own list.

Does it replace our ticketing?

No — it runs alongside it. It’s for the guest list: comps, artist and crew, industry, media and walk-ups.

What do door staff need?

Any phone or tablet with a browser. Add it to the home screen and it opens like an app.

Does it work without internet?

Not yet — door phones need Wi-Fi or mobile data. If one phone drops out, the others keep working and it catches up when it reconnects.

Can we bring in our existing list?

Yes. Paste straight from Excel, Google Sheets or an email — one name per line, with “+2” for plus-ones.
