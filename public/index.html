One login, names on every change

The whole venue shares one password. Each phone asks for a name once, so you always know who added, edited or let someone in.

Contributor links

Allocations counted in heads including plus-ones, automatic cutoffs, and links you can switch off or replace in one tap.

Check in and out

Partial plus-ones, re-entry and walk-ups. Live counts for who’s inside, who’s arrived and who hasn’t shown.

VIPs never wait

VIPs are highlighted gold on every door screen, and everyone gets an alert the moment one arrives.

Paste in any list

Copy a column straight out of Excel, Sheets or an email. “Jane Smith +2” just works. Export to CSV for settlement.

Private by design

Each venue only ever sees its own lists. Nothing is public, nothing is indexed by search engines, and there’s no tracking.

Get your venue on

Sign up your venue

Pick a username and password for your venue. Once we’ve approved it — usually the same day — you and your staff log in with those details.

  • One login for the venue. Staff share the username and password; each phone asks for their name once.

Questions

Do our staff need accounts?

No. Your venue has one password. Staff open your venue’s link, type the password, and put their name on the phone once.

Do artists and promoters need to sign up?

No. They get a private link for that show. They can add, edit and remove their own guests until your cutoff — and they only ever see their own list.

Does it replace our ticketing?

No — it runs alongside it. This is just for the guest list: comps, artist and crew, industry, media and walk-ups.

Does it work without internet?

Not yet. Door phones need Wi-Fi or mobile data. If one phone drops out, the others keep working and it catches up when it reconnects.

Can we bring in our existing list?

Yes. Paste straight from Excel, Google Sheets or an email — one name per line, with “+2” for plus-ones.
