Pricing is agreed with each venue directly. Until we agree a price in writing, use of the service is free, and either of us can stop at any time.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Logins. Keep the staff password, venue admin password and manager codes to the people who need them. Change them when staff leave. You are responsible for what happens under your venue’s login.
  • Contributor links. Anyone with a contributor’s link can add guests to that contributor’s allocation. Send links only to the right person, and switch a link off if it gets passed around.
